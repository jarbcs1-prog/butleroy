Butleroy

Your private voice assistant. Local-first, offline-ready, and completely under your control. Butleroy listens when spoken to, acts on your commands, and never phones home.

FeaturesDownload

Local-First Inference

All voice processing runs locally. Wake word detection, STT (faster-whisper), TTS (piper) — everything stays on your machine. No cloud API, no telemetry.

Always-Listening Wake Word

Butleroy listens for its wake word and only activates when spoken to. The wake word model is local and lightweight.

Full Voice Control

Say commands, ask questions, set reminders, control your environment. Butleroy understands natural language and executes actions locally.

Privacy-First

No cloud account, no data collection, no telemetry. Your voice and your data stay yours.

Architecture

Butleroy runs on your local machine with a Rust/Tauri front-end and a Python back-end. Voice is captured, wake word detected, STT decodes it locally, the assistant reasons, and TTS speaks the response — all in-process.

FAQ

Does Butleroy need internet?
No. All voice processing is local. Internet is only needed for optional third-party capabilities.
Is it really private?
Yes. No cloud account, no data collection, no telemetry. Everything runs locally.
How do I activate it?
Say the wake word. Butleroy will listen and respond to your commands.
